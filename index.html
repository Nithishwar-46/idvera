Category
Software
Theme
Smart Automation
Complete Problem Description
AICTE-approved institutions across India still manage student records through scattered, disconnected systems. Admission data, marksheets, attendance, scholarships and certificates sit in separate spreadsheets, ERPs and paper files. The same student can appear with different names, spellings or details in different places, and records are often duplicated or incomplete. Verifying a student’s identity, academic history or certificates is slow and manual. It involves emails, physical document checks and long approval chains, which opens the door to forged certificates, impersonation, duplicate admissions and fake scholarship claims. Institutions, regulators and employers have no single, trusted, real-time source of truth. Even though the APAAR ID gives every student a unique lifelong academic identity, there is no simple platform that lets institutions use it to unify, validate and verify student data at scale.

Our SolutionInnovation Pillars

A comprehensive architectural paradigm engineered to solve the core bottlenecks with efficiency and scale.

STEP 01

APAAR-Linked Central Repository

Institutions upload or sync student records via bulk upload (CSV/Excel) or API sync, mapping every record to the student's unique 12-digit lifelong APAAR ID.

PILLAR 01ACTIVE SPEC →
STEP 02

AI-Assisted OCR Verification

Tesseract and PaddleOCR engines extract text from marksheets and certificates, cross-referencing extracted credentials with stored institutional records to detect tampering.

PILLAR 02ACTIVE SPEC →
STEP 03

Sanctioned Intake Reconciliation

Automated audit engine compares actual enrolments against approved capacity across General, NRI, Supernumerary, and Lateral Entry seats to eliminate over-admissions.

PILLAR 03ACTIVE SPEC →
STEP 04

Cross-Institution Deduplication

System-wide matching detects concurrent full-time admissions of the same student across multiple colleges, flagging ghost enrollments and fake scholarship claims.

PILLAR 04ACTIVE SPEC →
STEP 05

Tamper-Proof Verification Trail

Generates SHA-256 cryptographic hashes for every verified record, issuing instant QR codes and shareable verification links for instant third-party validation.

PILLAR 05ACTIVE SPEC →
STEP 06

Role-Based Unified Dashboards

Tailored portals for Students (view & share verified profiles), Institution Admins (bulk data management & correction), and Regulators (real-time compliance & anomaly monitoring).
