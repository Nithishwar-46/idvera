Complete Problem Description
AICTE-approved institutions across India still manage student records through scattered, disconnected systems. Admission data, marksheets, attendance, scholarships and certificates sit in separate spreadsheets, ERPs and paper files. The same student can appear with different names, spellings or details in different places, and records are often duplicated or incomplete. Verifying a student’s identity, academic history or certificates is slow and manual. It involves emails, physical document checks and long approval chains, which opens the door to forged certificates, impersonation, duplicate admissions and fake scholarship claims. Institutions, regulators and employers have no single, trusted, real-time source of truth. Even though the APAAR ID gives every student a unique lifelong academic identity, there is no simple platform that lets institutions use it to unify, validate and verify student data at scale.
